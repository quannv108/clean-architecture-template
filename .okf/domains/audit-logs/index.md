The 4W audit trail slice. Start with the slice concept; the other files are its entity and errors.

# Concepts

* [audit-logs](audit-logs.md) - The 4W audit trail: who did what, when and where, recorded for endpoints marked for auditing.
* [AuditLogErrors](audit-log-errors.md) - Domain error factories for audit log validation failures.
* [AuditLog](audit-log.md) - One recorded action - who, what, when and where - immutable once created.
